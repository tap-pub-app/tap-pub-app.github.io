Delete your Tap account

You can delete your Tap account and the data that goes with it at any time.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open Tap and go to Profile, then Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This removes your profile, check-ins, photos, comments and friends. It can't be undone.

By email

If you can't get into the app, email tapapp.hello@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my Tap account. We'll delete your account and confirm by reply.
