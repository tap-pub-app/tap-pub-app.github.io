Who we are, and the short version

Tap is an app for adults to check in at pubs, bars, clubs and breweries. It is run by Angus Neale, a sole trader trading as Tap ("we", "us"), and we are the "data controller" of your information under UK GDPR.

The short version: we collect only what Tap needs to work: your account, your check-ins, your location at the moment you check in, and photos you choose to post. We do not sell your data, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and you can delete your account inside the app whenever you like.

Location

Tap uses your precise location only when you are using the app, and only to find venues near you and to confirm that you are close enough to check in.

  • When you check in, your phone's coordinates are sent to our server once so it can measure the distance to the venue. We do not save those coordinates with your check-in.
  • We do not track you in the background and we do not build a history of where you have been.
  • Other users see the venue you checked in at, not your exact position.
  • You can turn location off at any time in iPhone Settings, then Tap, then Location. You can still browse Tap, but you will not be able to check in.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your phone's push token so we can send you alerts about your account activity, such as friend requests, cheers and comments. The token and the alert are passed through Apple's push notification service to reach your phone, and are not shared with anyone else.

  • You can switch notifications off at any time in iPhone Settings, then Tap, then Notifications.
  • We remove your push token when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Tap also puts a number on its app icon showing how many unread items you have.

Photos, posts, and who can see what

Tap is social, so some of what you add is seen by other people. You control the most personal part: which pubs you have visited.

  • Always visible to other signed-in users: your username, profile picture, bio, profile style, status, and the badges you choose to show.
  • Your visited pubs and check-ins: you choose whether they are visible to everyone, to friends only, or to no one. Friends only means people whose friend request you accepted.
  • Your feed: you see your own check-ins and those of friends and people whose settings allow it. Blocked people never see you, and you never see them.
  • Comments and cheers on a check-in are visible to anyone who can see that check-in.
  • Leaderboards show your username and score. You can opt out of leaderboards in your settings.
  • Photos you add to a check-in are stored privately and shown to others through temporary links, only to people who can see that check-in.

Please do not post photos of other people without their say-so, and nothing that shows a child. Our Terms of Use cover this.

Pub owners and venue pages

Tap lets people who run a pub, bar, club or brewery ask to be registered as its owner or manager. If you don't run a venue, this section doesn't apply to you, except that you will see verified venue pages, events and promoted items.

If you ask to be registered, we collect your full name, your role (owner, manager, licensee or other), a phone number or email address, and a short note on how you are connected to the venue. Only our moderators can see these details. We use them to check your claim and to contact you about it, and we message you in the app when it is approved or declined. They are never shown to other users and never shared with anyone else.

Once you are registered, the venue's page shows a blue verified tick. Everything you add to the page is public to every Tap user: the logo, cover photo, tagline, bio, opening hours, website, phone number, Instagram name, features and events. Please don't add anything you wouldn't want the public to see. A "Pub owner" badge also appears on your own profile, like any other badge. It doesn't say which venue.

What owners can and can't see. When someone taps "I'm interested" on an event, we store that against their account, but owners only see the total, never who. Owners also see counts of check-ins at their venue (and, with Pub Pro, the busiest day and hour and a weekly trend). They never see who checked in.

Plans and promotion requests. If you ask for Pub Pro, a Featured Venue slot or an Event Spotlight, we store the request: the plan, the wording you wrote, any button link, the dates, your note, who sent it and what we decided. No payment is taken inside the app at the moment. When paid plans go through the App Store, Apple takes the payment and we never see your card details. Promoted items are labelled "Sponsored" or "Featured", and we check each one before it goes live.

Reports. Anyone can report a venue page or event. We store the report and who sent it, and treat it like other reports (see "How long we keep information").

Tap Plus waiting list. If you join the waiting list for our planned personal plan, we record that you joined, against your account, so we can tell you when it is ready. You can leave the list at any time on the same screen.

Keeping and deleting. We keep claim details until you delete your account. If you delete your account, we delete your claim, your owner link, your "interested" taps, your reports and your waiting-list entry. You stop being the owner, and if you were the only owner, the venue's page, events and promotions are removed too. Records of promotion requests are kept as business records, without your name or account attached.

Your rights, and deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app, at any time: tap Delete my account and confirm. Deleting your account removes your profile, check-ins, comments, friend connections and uploaded photos from Tap. Photo files can take up to 30 days to be fully cleared from storage. It cannot be undone.

Under UK GDPR you also have the right to:

  • get a copy of the information we hold about you
  • have wrong information corrected
  • have your information erased
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • take your information to another service in a usable format
  • withdraw consent, such as for location or notifications, at any time

To use any of these, email tapapp.hello@gmail.com. It is free, and we will reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm who you are first.

Age limit: 18 and over

Tap is only for people aged 18 or over, because it is about venues that serve alcohol. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it. If you think a child is using Tap, please tell us at tapapp.hello@gmail.com.

Security and international transfers

Your information is held on Supabase's secure cloud infrastructure. Connections between the app and our servers are encrypted, passwords are stored hashed, photos are kept private, and each user can only reach the data our access rules allow. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Some of our providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the UK's approved safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or an adequacy decision. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

Advertising and sponsored venues

Tap may show sponsored venues and adverts from venue partners in your feed. They are clearly labelled. Verified venue owners can ask for a promotion, and we approve each one and choose where it shows, usually by area. We do not use third-party ad networks, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not share your information with advertisers. Because of that, Tap does not ask for permission to track you.
