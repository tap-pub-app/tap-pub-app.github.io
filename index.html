Tap Support

Tap is an app for adults to check in at pubs, bars, clubs and breweries. If something is not working, or you have a question, we are happy to help.

Contact us

Email tapapp.hello@gmail.com. Please include your username and, if it is a bug, what you were doing and which iPhone and version of Tap you have. We read every message and reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

You can do it yourself in the app at any time: tap Delete my account and confirm. It removes your profile, check-ins, comments, friend connections and photos from Tap, and cannot be undone. Photo files can take up to 30 days to clear from storage.

Check-in says I am too far away, or location is off

You need to be near the venue to check in, and Tap needs permission to see your location while you use the app. Open iPhone Settings, then Tap, then Location and choose While Using the App with Precise Location on. We never track you in the background.

I am not getting notifications

Open iPhone Settings, then Tap, then Notifications and make sure Allow Notifications is on. If you tapped Don't Allow earlier, you can switch it on there. Make sure you are signed in and your phone is connected to the internet.

How do I report something or block someone?

You can report any check-in, comment or person from inside Tap, and you can block people from their profile. A moderator reviews every report, and the person you report is not told who reported them. If you need urgent help, email us.

A pub is missing or its details are wrong

Use the venue suggestion option in the app to send us a name, address and map pin. For wrong details on an existing venue, email us the venue name and what needs fixing.

I run a pub, bar, club or brewery

You can ask to be registered as the owner or manager of your venue from inside the app. We check each request before a venue page gets its blue tick. You can also email us.

I cannot sign in or did not get my confirmation email

Check your spam folder first. You can also use Sign in with Apple. If it still does not work, email us from the address you signed up with and we will sort it out.

Is Tap for under 18s?

No. Tap is only for people aged 18 or over, because it is about venues that serve alcohol. Please drink responsibly. If you are worried about your drinking, Drinkaware offers free, confidential help.
